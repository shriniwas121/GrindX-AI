Privacy Policy for Grindx AI

Effective date: 19 April 2026

Grindx AI (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides an AI-powered study assistant that helps users upload study materials, generate summaries, ask questions, create practice questions, and use mock tests and text-to-speech features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we share it, how long we keep it, and what choices users have.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

A. Account Information

When you create an account or sign in, we may collect:

Name
Email address
Authentication identifiers
Subscription and billing status
B. Study Content You Provide

When you use Grindx AI, we may collect content you actively submit, including:

Uploaded files such as PDF, Word, text, and image files
Pasted text
URLs submitted for analysis
Chat messages and questions
Document summaries and related generated study outputs
C. Usage Information

We may collect limited service usage information, such as:

Upload counts
Chat usage counts
Audio/listen usage counts
Timestamps related to activity
Basic app interaction data needed to operate the service
D. Billing Information

If you subscribe to a paid plan or start a trial, billing-related records may be processed through our payment provider, Stripe. We do not store your full card number in our app systems. We may store billing-related identifiers such as:

Stripe customer ID
Stripe subscription ID
Subscription status
Trial and plan end dates
Cancellation status
E. Minimal Anti-Abuse Retention

If you delete your account after using a free trial or paid subscription flow, we may retain a minimal record of your email address solely to help prevent repeated free-trial abuse and protect the integrity of our billing system. We do not retain deleted study content for this purpose.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

Create and manage user accounts
Authenticate users
Process uploaded study material
Generate summaries, key concepts, practice questions, mock tests, and AI responses
Provide text-to-speech and related study features
Sync documents and chat history across a user’s devices
Process subscriptions, billing, trials, cancellations, and account status
Enforce plan limits and prevent misuse
Maintain app security, reliability, and performance
Comply with legal obligations
3. How We Process Study Content

Study materials and chat content you submit are processed to deliver app functionality, including:

Document analysis
AI-generated answers
OCR and image-based text extraction
Language translation
Audio generation
Practice and mock test generation

This processing may involve trusted third-party service providers that help us deliver these features.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party providers to operate Grindx AI, including services for:

Authentication and database hosting
Subscription and payment processing
AI and language model processing
Speech and document intelligence features

These providers may process personal information only as needed to provide their services to us.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information:

With service providers that help us operate the app
With payment processors for subscription and billing operations
When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request
To protect the rights, safety, security, and integrity of Grindx AI, our users, or others
In connection with a business transfer, merger, acquisition, or asset sale
6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Account and Study Data

Documents, chat history, profile data, and related app data are kept while your account remains active, unless deleted earlier by you or us.

Deleted Documents and Chat

If you delete a document or clear chat history, the associated stored data is removed from our app systems in line with our deletion workflow.

Deleted Accounts

If you delete your account, your profile, documents, chat history, and related study data are removed from our application systems, subject to technical, legal, fraud-prevention, and billing-integrity needs.

Limited Retained Identifier

If applicable, we may retain a minimal email record solely to prevent repeated trial abuse and support billing integrity. We do not keep deleted study materials for that purpose.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

Access your personal information
Correct inaccurate information
Request deletion of your information
Withdraw consent where consent is used
Object to or restrict certain processing
Request information about how your data is handled

You may also:

Delete documents and chat history within the app
Delete your account through the app or by contacting us
Manage or cancel subscriptions through the available billing controls
8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children’s Privacy

Grindx AI is intended for general educational use. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable law, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

10. International Data Processing

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, depending on where our service providers operate. Where required, we take reasonable steps to protect personal information in line with applicable law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after an update means the updated Privacy Policy applies, to the extent permitted by law.
